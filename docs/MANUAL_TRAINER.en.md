# Mini Manual — Trainer Module

A practical guide for you, the training professional, to get the most out of the app
day to day: what each screen does, how to reach it, and what your license unlocks.

---

## 1. How you navigate

The app has a **bottom bar** (always visible) with five shortcuts:

| Icon | Name | What it's for |
|------|------|---------------|
| 👤 | **Clients** | Your home screen: client list, invitations and one-off sessions |
| ✨ | **Check-in** | Record the client's state (energy, pain, sleep, time) |
| 🔔 | **Inbox** | Safety alerts and notices from your clients |
| 🕘 | **History** | Timeline of workouts and sessions |
| ☰ | **Menu** | Access to Coach DNA, Library, Progress, Subscription and Settings |

Rule of thumb: almost everything starts in **Clients**. You open a client and, from
there, create workouts, run check-ins and review the scores.

---

## 2. The features, step by step

### Register and invite clients
**Clients → "Invite" button**. Enter the name and email; the client receives the
invitation and appears in your list as soon as they accept. How many clients you can
keep depends on your license (see section 3).

### Open a client's file
**Clients → tap the client's name**. You enter the file, where you see the readiness
history, the scores and the action buttons.

### Create a workout
**Clients → client → "Create workout"**. In the editor you can:
- **Add an exercise manually** ("Add") — type the name and the app suggests exercises
  from the library with sets, reps and load already filled in.
- **Generate with AI** ("Ask AI") — the app builds the workout from the client's
  profile and latest check-in, respecting the available time. The exercises are
  **added** to what you already entered, never replaced.
- **Send to the client** ("Send to client") — the workout is published and the client
  gets notified. If something fails during sending, the app now warns you on screen
  instead of failing silently.

### Run the client's check-in
**Clients → client → "Check-in for [name]"**. Use it before building the workout: the
energy, pain and sleep data feed the AI suggestion and the scores.

### Review readiness scores
Inside the client's file. The **basic score** shows the day's readiness level; the
**advanced score** breaks down the factors behind the number (available from Pro).

### Coach DNA — your methodology
**Menu → Coach DNA**. Here you record your training principles so the AI generates
plans with **your** identity, not generic ones. Available from Pro.

### Exercise library
**Menu → Exercise library**. Browse the catalog used by the workout editor's
suggestions.

### One-off session (no client registration)
**Clients → "One-off session"**. Creates a single workout for someone who isn't a
regular client — handy for a trial class. It doesn't count against your client limit.

### Switch license
**Menu → Subscription**. Compare the plans and upgrade at any time.

## 3. What each license unlocks

Three licenses, in increasing order of features: **Trial → Pro → Elite**.

| Feature | Trial | Pro | Elite |
|--------|:-----:|:---:|:-----:|
| Number of clients | up to **3** | up to **50** | **unlimited** |
| Create workout manually | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Readiness score (basic) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **AI workout generation** | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **AI check-in adjustment** | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Advanced score** (detailed factors) | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Coach DNA** (your methodology in the AI) | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Studio branding** | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Marketplace listing** | — | — | ✓ |
| **Marketplace revenue share** | — | — | ✓ |

How to read the table:
- **Trial** is for getting to know the app: you register up to 3 clients and build
  workouts manually, but without AI or Coach DNA yet.
- **Pro** is the working plan: it unlocks the AI (generation and adjustment), the
  advanced scores, Coach DNA and studio branding — enough to run a full client roster.
- **Elite** adds a presence in the **marketplace** (listing and revenue share), aimed
  at those who want to attract new clients through the platform.

## 4. Frequently asked questions

**"I can't create a workout."** Make sure you opened the client from the **Clients**
screen before tapping "Create workout" — the editor needs to know which client the
workout is for. When sending, if an error message appears, try again; the app now
reports the failure instead of freezing without notice.

**"The AI button doesn't show / doesn't work."** AI generation is available from
**Pro**. On Trial, build the workout manually or upgrade in **Menu → Subscription**.

**"I've reached the client limit."** Each license has its ceiling (3 / 50 / unlimited).
To expand, upgrade in **Menu → Subscription**.
