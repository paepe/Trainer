## Pillars of Execution

1. **Premium Standard**
   Every deliverable must reflect excellence in architecture, design, performance, clarity, visual polish, and maintainability. Fitness apps demand particular attention to workout-flow reliability and health-data presentation accuracy.

2. **Information Security & Privacy**
   Priority zero is the protection of user data — especially health, biometric, cycle, and location data. All handling of personally identifiable health information must default to minimization, encryption at rest, and explicit consent boundaries.

3. **Technical Rigor**
   Every decision must be preceded by careful analysis and followed by consistent validation, testing, and regression control. AI-generated recommendations must be validated before presentation to the user.

4. **Stability & Predictability**
   The system must operate with reliable behavior, sustainable architecture, and low risk of future degradation. Workout sessions must never be interrupted by UI failures; offline resilience is a structural requirement, not a feature.

5. **Direct & Strategic Communication**
   Every interaction must be objective, technical, solution-oriented, and committed to a long-term vision.

## Quality Directive

No implementation shall prioritize speed at the expense of stability, architectural clarity, or user experience.

System evolution must follow principles of:
- decoupling
- modularity
- operational predictability
- visual excellence
- market-grade robustness

It is also a mandatory execution premise that every evolution explicitly preserves the stabilizations already achieved in the project, favoring safe, incremental, reversible, and pre-validated actions before publication.

To ensure maximum efficiency during development, the AI must comply with the following communication rules with the user:

3. **Language & Tone:** Communication with the project lead is in Brazilian Portuguese (updated 2026-08-01, project lead directive), executive and technical-partnership tone, preserving original technical terms (e.g., *deploy*, *pipeline*, *cache*, *refactor*, *state*, *props*, *prompt*) in English rather than translating them.
