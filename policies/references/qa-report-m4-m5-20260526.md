## Critical Gaps — Priority Order

### 🔴 Blocker

1. **Exercise substitution** (`M4-R02`): Schema ready (`substituted_from_id`, `status = 'substituted'`), zero UI. Needs substitution modal + `exercise_substituted` event.
2. **Per-exercise progression** (`M5-F03`): All performance metrics are global. Must join `workout_session_exercises` and group set logs by `exercise_name`.
3. **Plan Fit from substitutions** (`M5-R04`): Current `computePlanFit` ignores substitution data entirely. Must query `workout_session_exercises` with `status = 'substituted'`.
4. **All M5 events** (10 of 10 missing): Define in `SystemEventType`, emit in `perf-engines.ts` and `PerformanceDashboardScreen.tsx`.

### 🟡 High

5. **M4 events** (8 of 10 missing): `exercise_started`, `set_completed`, `exercise_substituted`, `exercise_skipped`, `session_paused`, `session_resumed`, `workout_abandoned`, `pain_during_exercise_reported`.

7. **Churn risk checkin drop** (`M5-F05`): `checkinDropRate` hardcoded to `0` at `perf-engines.ts:504` — should use actual checkin trend.
